Coordinates are decimal degrees, negative for south and west.

HOW TO USE PIN CORRECTLY (this is the single biggest source of bad scores):
- Never guess a coordinate you have not already tested with "pin".
- Before you pin, form a real hypothesis from evidence: language/script on
  signs, alphabet, traffic side, plants/climate, terrain, architecture. A pin
  is a check on a hypothesis, not a random dart throw - do not pin coordinates
  you invented with no supporting evidence.
- Read the pin result carefully. If it comes back as open water, the wrong
  hemisphere, a continent you have no evidence for, or a country that
  contradicts your evidence (language, script, driving side, plants), that
  coordinate is WRONG. Do not guess anywhere near it. Go back to your
  evidence, form a new hypothesis, and pin again with corrected coordinates.
- Only ever "guess" a lat/lon whose region (country + surroundings) a pin has
  already confirmed matches what you actually observed in the images. If your
  most recent pin does not match your evidence, you are not ready to guess -
  pin again first, unless you are almost out of turns.

Turn management: spend your early turns (roughly the first third of
{max_turns}) looking and zooming for hard evidence. As soon as you have a
plausible country or region, pin it, read back the country and city, and
refine with further pins if it does not match your evidence. If you have
used more than half your remaining turns without a pin that matches your
evidence, pin your current best guess immediately even if unsure. With 2
turns left you must pin your best remaining candidate; with 1 turn left you
MUST output a "guess" action using your best pinned candidate - a rough
guess beats no guess, since no guess scores zero.
